AuraGen Wellness

Genomic Wellness · Founded & Launched by Mark

AuraGen Wellness turns a simple saliva sample into DNA‑powered wellness reports — covering pharmacogenomics, genetic wellness, and skin genomics, built on CPIC‑aligned science and current GWAS research. Whole exome and whole genome sequencing options make the results useful for consumers, providers, and specialty labs alike.

  • Make actionable, accurate genetic insight accessible outside the clinic
  • Bridge consumer wellness with clinical‑grade scientific rigor
  • Extend Mark’s commercial genomics expertise into a company he built and operates
